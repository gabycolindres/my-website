01

Reships

Sending a replacement at no charge so the customer ends up with what they ordered. Used when the product never arrived, arrived wrong, or arrived unusable.

When it qualifies

Lost in transit

No tracking movement for [X] business days, or carrier marks it lost.

Damaged on arrival

Product unusable due to shipping damage. Photo required.

Wrong item

Customer received a different item/variant than ordered.

Missing item

Part of a multi-item order didn't arrive.

Defective / quality

Manufacturing fault confirmed by photo or description.

Don't reship ifTracking shows delivered and it's within the wait window — follow the "marked delivered but not arrived" exception on the Refunds page instead.

Process · In Shopify

  1. Confirm the issue
    Check tracking + ask for a photo if damage/defect/wrong item. Save photos to the ticket.
  2. Verify the shipping address
    Open the original order → confirm the address is correct before resending.
  3. Duplicate the order
    More actions → Duplicate. Remove anything not being reshipped (e.g. keep only the damaged item).
  4. Set price to $0
    Mark as Paid outside Shopify or apply a 100% discount so the customer isn't charged.
  5. Tag & note
    Add tag reship + reason tag (e.g. damaged). Note the original order # in the internal note.
  6. Fulfill & send tracking
    Fulfill the new order and share the new tracking link with the customer.

What to say

Damaged / defective

Oh no — that's not the experience we want for you or your pup. I've sent a replacement [item] out to you at no charge, no need to return the damaged one. It'll arrive by [date]. Tracking: [link]

Lost in transit

Thanks for your patience — looks like this one got stuck with the carrier. Rather than keep you waiting, I've shipped a fresh [item] out today at no cost. You should see it by [date]. Tracking: [link]

Wrong / missing item

Apologies for the mix-up! You should've received [item]. I'm sending the correct one now at no charge — please keep what you received. Tracking: [link] — delivery by [date].

02

Refunds

Returning money to the customer's original payment method. Used when the customer doesn't want a replacement, when we can't fulfill, or as goodwill for a service failure.

When it qualifies

Cancelled pre-fulfillment

Order hasn't shipped and customer cancels. Full refund.

Return received

Item returned & inspected within the [X]-day window.

Can't fulfill

Out of stock / discontinued with no replacement.

Duplicate charge

Customer charged twice for one order.

Goodwill / partial

Service failure where a partial refund is the fair fix.

Approval neededRefunds over $[X], or refunds where the item is not being returned — ask your lead first.

Process · In Shopify

  1. Open the order
    Find it by order # or email and confirm payment status is Paid.
  2. Click Refund
    Top-right of the order → Refund.
  3. Choose amount
    Full or partial. For partials, enter the line-item qty or a custom amount. Shipping refunds are a separate flow — ask your lead.
  4. Set restock
    Toggle Restock items ON only if the product is coming back to inventory.
  5. Add reason & tag
    Internal reason note + tag refund and reason (e.g. return-received).
  6. Submit refund
    Refund goes to the original payment method. Typically lands in [5–10] business days.

What to say

Standard refund

All set — I've refunded [amount] to your original payment method. It usually takes [5–10] business days to show up depending on your bank.

Can't fulfill

I'm sorry — [item] is no longer available, so I won't leave you waiting on it. I've issued a full refund of [amount] back to your original payment method. Thanks for understanding.

Partial / goodwill

That shouldn't have happened, and I'd like to make it right. I've applied a [amount] refund to your order for the trouble while you keep the item. You'll see it in your account within [5–10] business days.

03

Store Credits

Credit toward a future purchase instead of cash back. Best when the customer is staying with us — use it as a generous gesture, never as a way to avoid a refund we owe.

When it qualifies

Customer prefers credit

They'd rather reorder than wait for a card refund.

Outside refund window

Past the return window but a goodwill gesture is warranted.

Inconvenience comp

Small credit to apologize for a delay or minor issue.

Loyalty gesture

Thank-you credit for a repeat or impacted customer.

Make it feel goodIf the customer is owed $[X], consider offering store credit of $[X + bonus] to tip the scales toward staying with us.

Process · In Shopify

  1. Open the customer profile
    Customers → search by name/email → open their profile.
  2. Add store credit
    Store credit section → Add credit. Enter the amount + reason.
  3. Note the reason
    Add a clear internal reason (e.g. "goodwill — late delivery, order #1234").
  4. Confirm expiry rules
    Check whether the credit expires per our policy and tell the customer if so.
  5. Tag the order
    Tag store-credit + reason so we can track usage.
Heads upIf our store uses gift cards instead of native store credit, generate one under Products and send the code directly to the customer.

What to say

Offering as an option

I can refund you, or — if you'd like — I can add [amount + extra] in store credit to your account so you've got a bit more to spend on the pups next time. What works best for you?

Credit applied

Done! I've added [amount] in store credit to your account. It'll apply automatically at checkout next time you're logged in. [Mention expiry if applicable]

Goodwill gesture

Thanks for sticking with us through this. As a small thank-you, I've added [amount] in store credit to your account for your next order. Hope it makes up for the wait.
